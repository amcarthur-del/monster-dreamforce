Know Before You Go

Conference Essentials
🪪

Badge & Registration

  • Pick up your badge at Moscone South Registration (open Sep 14, 3–7 PM for early pickup)
  • Bring a government-issued photo ID matching your registration name
  • Badge is required for all sessions, expo access, and evening events
  • Lost badge? Visit any Registration desk — bring ID
📍

Getting There

  • Fly into SFO or OAK — both ~30 min to downtown
  • BART: Powell St. or Montgomery St. stations, 5-min walk to Moscone
  • Ride-share drop-off: Howard St. entrance (avoid peak keynote congestion)
  • Parking: not recommended — limited and expensive (~$60/day)
  • Official travel page ↗
📱

Must-Have Apps

  • Salesforce Events App — personalized agenda for Jonathan & Brent, session builder, maps, schedule (iOS + Android) — download and log in before you arrive
  • Slack — team channel for real-time coordination
  • Salesforce Maps — navigate the Moscone campus
  • Add sessions to your calendar via the app before they fill up
👔

What to Wear

  • Business casual is standard — branded Monster gear is great for identity
  • Comfortable shoes are essential — expect 10,000+ steps/day across campus
  • Layers: Moscone sessions run cold; outdoor plazas can be warm in September
  • Evening events often have dress codes — check each invite
🗺️

Venue Layout

  • Moscone South — keynotes, registration, main expo hall
  • Moscone North — breakout sessions, Agentforce zone
  • Moscone West — partner pavilion, hands-on labs
  • SIC Location — confirm with your Salesforce host (typically offsite or dedicated floor)
💡

Pro Tips

  • Book popular sessions in the app now — they sell out weeks before the event
  • Keynotes fill fast — arrive 30+ min early for good seating
  • The expo hall is best early morning (9–11 AM) before crowds
  • Bring business cards or use the app's digital badge scanner
  • Charge your phone the night before — outlets are scarce on the floor
  • Dreamfest — Wed Sep 16, doors open 6:00 PM at Oracle Park (The Ballpark), 24 Willie Mays Plaza · included with Full Conference registration
  • Free cert exams — available throughout the event (one per attendee); bring Trailblazer login + government ID
